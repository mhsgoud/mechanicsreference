Linear momentum

A continuous body carries mass. Surface forces act on its current boundary; body forces act in the interior. Their resultant equals the rate of change of linear momentum. Locally that statement is Cauchy’s first law.

Cauchy’s first law

Red arrows are tractions on the current surface. They move with the body because Cauchy stress is a spatial field: the force is per current area, not per reference area.

∇ · σ + ρ b = ρ a
t = σ n
  • t = σ n Surface traction. The red arrows. By the divergence theorem this becomes ∇ · σ in the interior.
  • ρ b Body force per current volume — weight, or any force not acting through the surface.
  • ρ a Inertia. The cyan points are material particles; as they accelerate, the right-hand side is not zero.
