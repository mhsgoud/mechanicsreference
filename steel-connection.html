Setup

  • Tessellation from structural steel connection 3d model.stl (welded to 3958 unique vertices)
  • Column along $Z_{\mathrm{STL}}$ shown upright in Three.js $Y$
  • Fin plate on the $-X$ face at mid-height — a shear-tab / fin-plate joint
  • Base clamped. Gravity shear $V=250\,\mathrm{kN}$ on the tab; eccentricity $e=250\,\mathrm{mm}$ racks the panel zone

What the motion is

  • Column: finite-rotation beam (sections stay rigid). Mild drift, not a banana curve
  • Fin plate: in-plane parallelogram shear about the weld — the real shear-tab mode
  • Column web at the joint: panel-zone racking; flanges stay straighter
  • Scale multiplies the angles then rebuilds the pose, so exaggeration does not stretch the plates
