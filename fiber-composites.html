A ply and a laminate

Turn the fibre angle of a unidirectional ply. Stiffness along the plate axis x is E1 at 0°, near E2 at 90°, and strongly coupled to shear near 45°. The stack on the right is a laminate: classical lamination theory integrates those rotated plies through the thickness.
