Poisson contraction during uniaxial tension

Poisson’s ratio is −(lateral strain)/(longitudinal strain). Pull the specimen along its length: the dashed outline is the undeformed shape, and the solid outline necks under lateral contraction −ν ε. As ν → ½ the volume change vanishes and a displacement-only mesh locks.

ν = − εlat / εlong
