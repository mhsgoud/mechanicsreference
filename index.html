Three foundations

The same three questions run through the work: how a body is described, how that description is discretized, and how the discrete system is solved.

01

Continuum mechanics

Strain measures motion; stress measures internal force. Mass and momentum must balance, and a constitutive model closes the theory—before any mesh is chosen.

 02

Finite elements

Shape functions on a mesh replace the strong form with virtual work. Whether the discrete model is faithful depends on the element, the mesh, and the boundary data.

 03

Numerical methods

Quadrature, Newton iteration, and sparse solvers decide whether a formulation that is correct on paper can be solved at all—on CPU or on GPU.
