Deformation gradient

A motion maps material points X to spatial points x = χ(X). The deformation gradient F = ∂x/∂X is the linear part of that map: it sends a material line element to its image. Rigid translation never appears in F.

Homogeneous motions

x = χ(X),   F = ∂x / ∂X
