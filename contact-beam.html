Physical setup

  • Rigid wall and clamp plates fix both cantilevers at X = −50.
  • Upper arm (slave) and lower arm (master) share the wall.
  • Tip loads FY = −40 kN on seven slave nodes at the free end.
  • Initial clearance 1.25 mm; contact closes near the tip.

What to notice

  • Both arms bend — this is not a rigid foundation.
  • Tip U₂ ≈ −15.2 mm at the load nodes (λ = 1); field min ≈ −15.6 mm.
  • The F–δ curve softens until contact (~λ = 0.02), then stiffens as the patch grows.
  • Mesh shape at intermediate λ is λ·U(λ=1) — the curve itself is the true N–R path.
  • Compare with the smooth Hertz elliptic pressure.
