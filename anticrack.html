Assumptions and analytical basis

Infinite isotropic solid in antiplane elasticity, with z = x + iy and a = 1 in normalized coordinates. Take F(z) = √(z² − 1), choosing F ∼ z at infinity. For remote shear (A, B), the crack has w = A x + B Im F; the rigid line has w = A Re F + B y (μ = 1). Stresses follow from μ∇w. This comparison is not a reconstruction of an in-plane finite-element figure. Dal Corso, Shahzad & Bigoni: singularities and neutrality.
