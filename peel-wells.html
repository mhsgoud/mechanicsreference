Specimen (Sec. 6.2 / MATLAB)

  • L = 10 mm, plies 0.5 + 0.5 mm, plane strain
  • Fine mesh: 80 × 6 Q4 per ply (~960 elements)
  • Left x = 0 fully fixed; right corners uy = ±u
  • Starter crack a0 = 1 mm, traction-free, from the right
  • L–L: large-displacement interface + neo-Hookean bulk

Cohesive law (Wells Sec. 5)

  • ft = 1 MPa · Gf = 0.05 N/mm
  • Dummy toe (2% of Gf): gn0 = 0.002 mm, kn = 500 MPa/mm
  • Then exponential softening; released when κ ≥ −ln(0.02) Gf/ft
  • P–u is the MATLAB L–L fine history; the mesh is a large-rotation peel with the same CZM
