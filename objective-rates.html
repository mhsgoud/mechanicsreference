Objective rates: the two observers

The red arrows represent constant uniaxial tension attached to a rigidly rotating material block. Fixed global components change; components in the rotating material frame and the objective stress rate remain constant.

Why do we need objective rates?

The block undergoes rigid rotation only. The fixed observer sees changing stress components; the observer rotating with the material sees constant tension and zero physical stress rate.
