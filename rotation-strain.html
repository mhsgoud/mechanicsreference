A rotation is not a strain

Both squares undergo the same rigid rotation F = R. The right square is drawn with that rotation: Green strain is zero. The left square is drawn with I + ε, where ε is the infinitesimal strain of the same R. It changes shape. That error is why finite strain exists.

F = R   E = ½(FᵀF − I) = 0
