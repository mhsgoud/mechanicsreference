How the composite answers the fibre

Uniaxial tension along x. Rotate the fibres in the xy-plane. The reduced fibre is unstrained at an angle that depends only on the matrix Poisson ratio; there the effective Young’s modulus is that of the matrix. A solid fibre is neutral only once its diameter is small compared with the box. The plots follow the structure of Goudarzi and Simone, Int. J. Solids Struct. 156–157 (2019).
